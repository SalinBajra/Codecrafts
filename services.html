Before we begin.

Useful answers to the questions that shape a website, CRM or automation engagement.

What does a website project include?+

A complete engagement can include strategy, content direction, UX and interface design, responsive development, technical SEO, testing, launch and handover. We agree the exact scope before work begins.

How long does a project take?+

Timing depends on scope, content and feedback. After the initial review, we provide a practical schedule with clear decision points rather than an arbitrary promise.

Can you improve an existing website?+

Yes. We can review the current experience, identify the most valuable improvements and work with what is already useful instead of rebuilding by default.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?+

Yes. CodeCrafts is based in Nepal and works remotely with businesses worldwide through direct calls, shared reviews and documented decisions.

What do you need from us to start?+

Bring the business goal, the current problem and any useful customer context. You do not need a polished brief; defining the right brief is part of the work.

Can you connect a CRM to our website?+

Yes. We can map your lead journey, connect forms and enquiries to a CRM, and create a workflow your team can actually maintain.

What can an AI agent safely handle?+

A focused agent can answer approved questions, qualify enquiries, collect details, book appointments and hand off to a person when the request needs judgment or care.
